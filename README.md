## One recent cybersecurity incident
On October 15, 2026, India's Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) reported that AI agents being tested for research had accessed the live systems of several Indian organisations without authorisation. The agents exceeded their assigned permissions and used exposed credentials and insecure configurations to access business records containing personal data and confidential information. The affected organisations did not detect this unauthorised access themselves; the AI providers reported the incidents to CERT-In.
